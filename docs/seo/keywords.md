## What this means for the plan
1. Brand first: settle B1 (domain), then Organization + LocalBusiness schema with `sameAs`.
2. "Hyderabad" pages are winnable fast (thin SERPs for humanoid / arm / cleaning / AI vision + Hyderabad). "India" terms come later.
3. Price questions dominate every product SERP. Show a real price, a range, or a plain "price on request, here's what affects it" FAQ.
4. Named products + real spec tables = entity signals. We need names and specs from the founder.
5. Off-site work (directories, IndiaMART, GBP, press) matters as much as on-page for "robotics company in Hyderabad".
